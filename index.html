03 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

A player’s curiosity.
A developer’s mindset.

“The reason I make videogames is the same as why I play them: because it’s fun.”

I’m a 23-year-old game developer based in Hedehusene, Denmark. I enjoy being involved throughout development, learning as I go, and collaborating with artists to bring a shared idea to life.

My focus is gameplay mechanics and systems: getting the pieces working, making them feel good, and polishing the experience until it’s clear and enjoyable.

Away from the editor, you’ll find me enjoying trading card games and collectibles, acting and narration, or volleyball.

After my internship, I plan to apply for master’s programmes and continue improving my personal games.
